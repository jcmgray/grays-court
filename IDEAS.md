# Ideas

Things to try in the 3d theme, one at a time. They add detail where pieces
meet, and leave the pieces themselves as they are.

## Castles

- a short cobbled path from a castle's gate to the nearest road or farm, with
  a lantern post that glows at night
- a low outer wall or fence round the nearest corners of a castle's farms,
  when all its neighbours in the region are farms
- a flag in the region's crop colour once both its castles are built
- a smithy, with a forge that glows at night, on a farm between two castles

## River

- stepping stones across where a road runs beside the river
- a little boat moored by a mill or riverside hut
- reeds and lily pads gathering on the inside of bends
- a tiny waterfall where the river runs off the edge of the board
- ripples that slowly flow along the river (needs the view to keep redrawing,
  so check the cost on a phone)

## Hedges and fields

- a small wooden gate where two farms meet across a hedge
- a few sheep or cows instead of crops on one strip of a farm with only clear
  tiles round it
- haystacks on farms beside a road
- scarecrows on farms out in the open, with no hedge, castle or river beside
  them

## Roads

- a signpost or wayside cross where a road turns a corner
- a stone bridge over the river, or a gap through a hedge, if roads ever cross
  between regions
- a coaching inn on a farm that two roads pass

## Puzzle progress

- smoke from the chimneys of a region's huts once the region is complete
- when the puzzle is solved, lights rippling through every window at night,
  or bunting strung between the castles

## First to try
